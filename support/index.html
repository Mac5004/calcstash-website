CalcStash Support

Questions about using CalcStash? We're here to help.

How do I save a number?

Use the app's saved-number feature to give a number a name and, optionally, a note. You can access it again from My Numbers.

Where are my saved numbers?

CalcStash saves your numbers and calculation history locally on your device. There is no CalcStash account or cross-device synchronization in the current version.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored CalcStash data. Keep a separate record of anything important before uninstalling or resetting your device.

How do I report an issue or request a feature?

Email support@calc-stash.com. Include your app version, device model, and a brief description of the problem. Please do not send sensitive financial or personal information.
