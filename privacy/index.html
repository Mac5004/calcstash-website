Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CalcStash handles information when you use the CalcStash mobile application or contact us for support.

Information stored in the app

CalcStash is designed to store saved numbers, optional labels and notes, and calculation history locally on your device. The current app does not require you to create an account to use these features. CalcStash does not provide an account-based cloud synchronization service for this information.

Support communications

If you contact us at support@calc-stash.com, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond to your inquiry and provide support. Email communications are handled by our email service provider and may be subject to that provider's processing practices.

Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. As with most hosted websites, the hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request information, to deliver the website and maintain security. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Third-party services

Some operating-system services and third-party software components may process technical information as part of providing the app or distributing it through the App Store. This policy does not claim that Apple or other service providers collect no information. We will update these disclosures as needed if app functionality or service providers change.

Data retention and deletion

You can manage saved numbers and calculation history using available app controls. Uninstalling CalcStash may permanently remove information stored locally by the app. To request deletion of information you have sent to our support address, email us at the address below; we may retain information when reasonably necessary for legal or security purposes.

Children's privacy

CalcStash is a general-purpose calculator and is not designed specifically for children. Please do not send children's personal information to our support email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when CalcStash changes. The updated policy and effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@calc-stash.com.
